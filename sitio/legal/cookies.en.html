This site sets no cookies

Not "only essential cookies". Not "cookies you can opt out of". None. That is why there is no consent banner on this site: there is nothing to ask you about.

The site also writes nothing to your browser's local storage, session storage or databases. Your bag measurements, your chosen language and your unit preference live in the page's memory while the tab is open and are gone the moment you close it.

Don't take our word for it — check

This is the rare claim on a privacy page that you can verify yourself in about fifteen seconds, and we would rather you did.

  1. Press F12 to open your browser's developer tools, or right-click the page and choose Inspect.
  2. Open the Application tab in Chrome or Edge, or Storage in Firefox or Safari.
  3. Look under Cookies, Local storage and Session storage for willitboard.com.

All three should be empty. If they are not, something has changed that we have not documented, and we would like to know: hello@willitboard.com.

The one third party, and what we can't promise about it

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google's servers. That request is the only thing that leaves this site, and it is described in full on the privacy page.

We are deliberately not telling you what Google does or does not store as a result of it. We can verify what our own site does, because we build it. We cannot verify another company's internal practices, and a cookie page that confidently paraphrases a third party's behaviour is exactly the kind of secondhand claim this project exists to avoid. So we link Google's own privacy notes for Google Fonts and let Google speak for Google.

Serving the fonts from our own domain would remove this last third party altogether. It is on the list.

If this ever changes

Advertising and affiliate programmes generally do use cookies. Willitboard runs neither today. If either arrives, this page will be rewritten to name every cookie, who sets it, what it is for and how long it lasts — and it will be rewritten before the first one is set, not after. If consent is required, you will be asked for it properly, and declining will still leave you a working bag checker.
