This site sets no cookies

Not "only essential cookies". Not "cookies you can opt out of". None. That is why there is no consent banner on this site: there is nothing to ask you about.

The site writes exactly one thing to your browser, and only if you ask for it. If you switch to night mode, that choice is kept in the browser's session storage under the name wib-theme, so the next page — or the other language — opens in night mode too. It is never sent anywhere, switching back to light deletes it, and closing the tab deletes it as well. Nothing else is stored: your bag measurements, your chosen language and your unit preference live in the page's memory while the tab is open and are gone the moment you close it.

Don't take our word for it — check

This is the rare claim on a privacy page that you can verify yourself in about fifteen seconds, and we would rather you did.

  1. Press F12 to open your browser's developer tools, or right-click the page and choose Inspect.
  2. Open the Application tab in Chrome or Edge, or Storage in Firefox or Safari.
  3. Look under Cookies, Local storage and Session storage for willitboard.com.

Cookies and local storage should be empty. Session storage should be empty too, unless you turned on night mode: then it holds that single entry, wib-theme, set to dark. If you find anything else, something has changed that we have not documented, and we would like to know: hello@willitboard.com.

No third parties either

Everything the page needs — the icons, the images and the operator data — is served from this site's own domain. Until 26 September 2026 the typefaces came from Google Fonts; now the text uses your device's own typeface, so no font is downloaded. The only thing your browser requests outside this domain is Cloudflare's visit counter, from the same company that hosts the site, and it sets no cookies and writes nothing to browser storage. The privacy page describes the host and the counter.

If this ever changes

Advertising and affiliate programmes generally do use cookies. Willitboard runs neither today. If either arrives, this page will be rewritten to name every cookie, who sets it, what it is for and how long it lasts — and it will be rewritten before the first one is set, not after. If consent is required, you will be asked for it properly, and declining will still leave you a working bag checker.
