Privacy

What this site knows about you, in full. The short answer is nothing, and this page explains why that is a fact about how it is built rather than a promise.

The short version

Willitboard collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-up, no form, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The site sets no cookies. The measurements you type never leave your browser.

That is not a promise about our intentions. It is a description of how the site is built: it is a set of static files with no server behind them and no database anywhere. There is no place for your data to go, because there is nothing running that could receive it.

What happens when you check a bag

When you type your bag's length, width, height and weight, the comparison runs inside your own browser against a single file of operator limits that your browser downloaded along with the page. Nothing is sent back to us. Nothing is written to your device. When you close the tab, the numbers are gone.

The same is true of the language you pick and the units you switch between. We don't remember them, because we have nowhere to remember them.

Who does see your IP address

Two companies receive your IP address when you open this site. Neither is because we asked for information about you — both are a consequence of how any page reaches any browser.

  • Cloudflare hosts the site. Like any web host, it has to receive your IP address in order to send you the page you asked for. What Cloudflare does with that is governed by Cloudflare's own privacy policy.
  • Google serves the typefaces this site uses. Your browser requests those font files from Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address and the fact that the request came from this site. What Google does with that is governed by Google's privacy policy and its privacy notes for Google Fonts. We link those rather than summarise them, because we can't verify another company's data practices from our side and we won't restate them as if we could.

This is the only part of the site that involves a third party at all, and we would rather it didn't. Serving the fonts from our own domain would remove it entirely, and that is on the list of things to change.

Cookies

This site sets none. The cookie page explains that in full, including how to check it yourself rather than take our word for it.

Analytics

There are none today — no Google Analytics, no pixel, no heatmap, no session recording. If we ever add a way of counting visits, we will pick one that does not build profiles of individual people, and this page will say what it is before it starts running, not after.

Advertising and affiliate links

There are none today. If that changes, advertising will involve third parties who do set cookies and do collect data, and both this page and the disclosure page will be rewritten to say exactly who and what before anything runs.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights over personal data that a site holds about you: to see it, correct it, have it deleted, and object to how it is used. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to show you, correct or delete. We are not saying this to avoid the obligation; we are saying it because the honest answer to "what do you have on me?" is "nothing".

The data that Cloudflare and Google receive as described above is held by them, under their own policies, which are linked in that section. If we ever begin holding personal data ourselves, this page changes first.

Children

The site is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Contact

Questions about this page, or about anything on the site, go to hello@willitboard.com.

Changes to this page

When this page changes, the date at the bottom changes with it, and we will not quietly widen what we collect. The pattern on the rest of this site is that every claim carries the date somebody checked it; this page works the same way.
