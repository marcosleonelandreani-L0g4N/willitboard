Stena Line: equipaje permitido

Stena Line describe qué se puede llevar en lugar de publicar medidas. Su regla se muestra abajo en sus propias palabras, y nunca se convierte en un sí o un no.

Qué dice Stena Line

Stena Line, en sus propias palabras — texto original en inglés, sin traducir

If you are travelling by car there is no limit to the amount of luggage you can carry in your vehicle as it will remain in your vehicle on our car deck. For foot passengers travelling to Sweden, Denmark, Germany, Latvia and Poland, there is no limit on the amount of luggage you can bring with you, however you need to be able to carry it onboard yourself. For foot passengers travelling to Ireland and to the UK from Ireland, there is no limit to the amount of luggage you can bring with you, however, hand luggage must be no larger than 60cm x 50cm x 40cm and you must be able to carry it yourself. If you are travelling with luggage that is larger than this it must be checked-in prior to boarding. Your luggage will be safely stowed during the sailing and can be collected upon arrival in the terminal. You will not be able to access your luggage during the sailing so please ensure you remove all necessary items before checking it in. If you are travelling by foot or by Rail & Sail on our Harwich-Hook of Holland route, you may take as much luggage as you like, as long as you can carry it onboard yourself. Passengers travelling on a day trip on our Irish Sea routes may carry one piece of small hand luggage. Any additional pieces of luggage will be charged at our discretion.

Antes de fiarse de esto

Un campo vacío en esta página significa que el operador no publica esa cifra. Nunca significa que no haya límite.

Cumplir un límite publicado no es una promesa de que el personal vaya a aceptar tu maleta en la puerta de embarque. Al menos un operador declara que pagar en la puerta no garantiza que se transporte una maleta que supere el límite.
